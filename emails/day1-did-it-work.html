Did it work? If not, it's probably one of these.

Most first builds hit one of three snags. None of them mean you did it wrong.

1

The button does nothing

Open it in Chrome or Edge. Firefox and Safari don't have the built-in speech recognition. In those, use the free-key version from step 2 instead.
2

It says "not-allowed", or never asks for the microphone

You probably double-clicked the file. Browsers only let speech recognition use your mic on a real web address. In VS Code, install the free Live Server extension and click Go Live: your page opens at 127.0.0.1, which counts.
3

It works, but misses your first word

That's normal for version 1, and it's the best lesson in it. Wait half a second after you press before you talk. Version 2 fixes it properly with a memory buffer, the same trick the paid apps use.
