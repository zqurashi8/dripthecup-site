The idea behind every good dictation app, in two minutes.

One model hears you. Another one understands you.

Here's the idea that separates version 1 from the apps people pay for. There are two jobs, and good tools use a different model for each.

Models that hear

Turn sound into words. Whisper is the best-known family. The tiny ones run on almost anything and miss names; the big ones catch accents and jargon but want a graphics card.

  

Models that understand

Turn words into what you meant: punctuation, no ums, "ten thirty" becomes 10:30. A small model on your own computer is free and private. A big cloud model is better at tone, and costs a little every time.

Try it tonight

If you added the free key in step 2, press Clean up and watch the token count. Tokens are how cloud models bill. Look up a model's price per million tokens and work out a month of your dictation: cents with a small model, a couple of dollars with a big one. So most of the $15 a month isn't paying for the model. It pays for everything stacked on top, and that's what the layers are.
