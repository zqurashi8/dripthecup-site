AI didn't take my job. It got me promoted.

I've been an integrations engineer for ten years. The job is making systems talk to each other, and taking manual work off people's plates.

A couple of years ago, every request that landed on my desk started the same way: can we put AI on this? So on my own time, I built small proofs of concept. Nothing big. I showed them working, and they became the projects I was asked to focus on.

That's the whole method, and it works anywhere: be curious, build small things that work, show the result. Everyone has the same AI now. What sets you apart is doing the extra: the 10x version of whatever you hand in.
