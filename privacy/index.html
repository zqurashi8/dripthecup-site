Privacy, in plain words

Updated October 3, 2026

This page covers dripthecup.com: the sign-up forms on /learn, the day one lab, the business request form and the shop's pre-order list. It's short because we collect very little.

What we collect

  • Your email address, when you sign up or reserve something.
  • What you choose to tell us: the optional note on a form, and for a business request, your company and what you want automated.
  • Which form you used (for example the day one lab or the founding list), and your country, which comes from the connection.
  • A scrambled code made from your internet address, kept for a day, only to stop one computer from flooding the forms. We never store the address itself.

The day one lab's scorecard and your dictation history stay in your own browser. They never reach us.

What we do with it

  • Send you what you asked for: the welcome email, the next version of the lab, the roadmap, and a note when the Lab opens.
  • Reply to a business request.
  • Count sign-ups, so we know what's working.

We don't sell your email, rent it, or share it with advertisers. Ever.

Who handles it

  • Cloudflare runs the website and stores the list.
  • Google (Gmail) carries the emails we send you from our Drip the Cup address.

Leaving, and deleting

Every email has a one-click unsubscribe link at the bottom. To have your details deleted completely, reply to any email from us and say so, and it's done within a few days.

Questions

Reply to any email from us, or use the business request form on /learn.
