Voice to text

How voice to text works: it hears numbers, not words

What it covers

Drip asks how voice to text knows what he's saying. It doesn't get words: his one question arrives as 45,600 numbers (2.85 seconds at 16,000 a second), gets chopped into slices and stacked into a grid of how loud each note is, and a model guesses the words that most likely made it. Every number and grid in the video is measured from the video's own audio. Then the part newer AI dictation apps add: punctuation, and cutting the slip when you correct yourself.
