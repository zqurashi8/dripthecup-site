Before you ask

Straight answers.

Why is the first part free?

Because the fastest way into this field is someone showing you the path, and the free course should stand on its own. The paid group is there for after, if you want live help and projects. I do make money from it, and I want you to make money too. That's the win-win.

Do I need to know how to code?

Level 0 assumes nothing. By level 1 you'll be running small Python scripts, with every line explained in plain English. If you already code, skip ahead: the enterprise level and the portfolio projects are where you'll spend your time.

What's free and what's paid?

Free: every video, the roadmap, the tools directory, the first build, and emails from me with the next step. Paid, and optional: the Lab, which adds live calls, reviews, the portfolio projects and the people. Nothing free gets taken away.

Is this a course on making Drip videos?

No. Drip is one level inside the Lab, taught as a case study: how a one-person AI pipeline scripts, voices, animates and posts a character, what the law says about disclosure, ownership, voices and sponsorships, and how a company would run the same thing. The rest of the Lab is enterprise AI and automation.

Will this get me a job?

I can't promise that, and anyone who does is selling something. I genuinely want you to get one. I'll show you what the job actually is, what I built to get ahead in mine, and how to put together a portfolio that proves you can do it.

Is any of this from your employer?

No. Everything here is built on my own time, with my own tools, on public data and my own projects. Nothing from work is in it and nothing from work ever will be.

When does the Lab open?

Soon. You don't need it to start: sign up above and I'll send you the free path now, and tell you when the Lab opens.

Why Skool?

One place for the classroom, the feed and the calendar, with levels built in. You'll get a link when it opens; there's nothing to install.
