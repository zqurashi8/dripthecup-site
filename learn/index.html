How this works

AI is free. The plan is the hard part.

Before you ask

Straight answers.

Why is the first part free?

Because the information should be free. What I charge for in the Lab is the structure: the full builds, a plan for your level, and live help. The free path stands on its own either way.

Do I need to know how to code?

Level 0 assumes nothing. By level 1 you'll be running small Python scripts, with every line explained in plain English. If you already code, skip ahead: the enterprise level and the portfolio projects are where you'll spend your time.

What's free and what's paid?

Free: every video, the roadmap, the tools directory, the first build, levels 0, 1, 2 and 4, and emails from me with the next step. Paid, and optional: the Lab, with the full builds on this page, their code, live calls and reviews. Nothing free gets taken away.

Are there certificates, a lifetime deal, or upsells?

No. No certificates, no lifetime tier, no upsells inside the group. One price, monthly or yearly, and you can cancel any time. What you show an employer is what you built.

Is this a course on making Drip videos?

No. Drip is one level, taught as a case study, plus a side quest on making and editing videos with AI. The rest is enterprise AI and automation.

Will this get me a job?

I can't promise that, and anyone who does is selling something. I genuinely want you to get one. I'll show you what the job actually is, what I built to get ahead in mine, and how to put together a portfolio that proves you can do it.

Do you work with businesses?

Yes. I set up AI systems and workflows for small teams. Projects start at $500, with a fixed quote after a free 20-minute call. Ask through the form in Work with me.

Is any of this from your employer?

No. Everything here is built on my own time, with my own tools, on public data and my own projects. Nothing from work is in it and nothing from work ever will be.

When does the Lab open?

Soon. You don't need it to start: sign up above and I'll send you the free path, and tell you when the Lab opens.

Why Skool?

One place for the classroom, the feed and the calendar, with levels built in. You'll get a link when it opens; there's nothing to install.
