Before you ask

Straight answers.

Do I need to know how to code?

Level 0 assumes nothing. By level 1 you'll be running small Python scripts, with every line explained in plain English. If you already code, skip ahead: the enterprise level and the portfolio projects are where you'll spend your time.

What's the difference between free and paid?

The free path is the start, and it stands on its own: the dictation lab, the roadmap, the AI tools directory, my public videos, and emails from me with the next step. The Lab is the structure on top: the full builds and their code, deeper videos made only for members, a plan for your level, live calls and reviews. The information should be free. What you pay for is the plan and the help.

Will this help me get a job?

I can't promise that, and anyone who does is selling something. I genuinely want you to get one. I'll show you what the job actually is, what I built to get ahead in mine, and how to put together a portfolio that proves you can do it.

Do you work with businesses?

Yes. I've spent ten years building enterprise integrations and workflow automations, and I set up AI systems and workflows for businesses. Projects start at $1,000, with a fixed quote after a free 20-minute call. Pick Work with me at the top of this page.
